# Exercise catalogue

The 179 exercises in the app's [StarterExercises.kt](https://github.com/scttech/open_fitness/blob/main/app/src/main/java/com/scttech/android/kotlin/openfitness/domain/template/StarterExercises.kt) (a copy is in `exports/StarterExercises.kt.txt`), each with what it would take to animate with the library from [Step 17](17-the-exercise-recipe.md).

The name, category and equipment come from the app. **The status column is our judgement, made from the exercise names and without trying them.** Treat it as a first sort; it is generated by `tools/make_catalog.py`, so correct it there.

## Status

| Status | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Built** | 19 | done: an action exists in the Blender file and passes the checks (from the text files in tools/exercises/ or the earlier Python tables) |
| **Variant** | 0 | an existing table with changed numbers or one changed grab; no new technique |
| **New** | 53 | a new table, using techniques and props we already have |
| **Fixture** | 13 | the motion is easy; it needs a bench, box, wall, chair or parallel bars modelled first (a static prop with no bones) |
| **Dumbbell** | 18 | needs a dumbbell prop with grips (two hands, or one), and a bench for some |
| **Barbell** | 17 | needs a barbell prop with a two-hand grip, plus a rack or bench for most |
| **Machine** | 17 | needs a machine or cable fixture modelled; the person mostly sits or stands still |
| **Small prop** | 10 | needs a band, rope, wheel, ball or roller; bands and ropes bend, which a rigid prop bone cannot do |
| **Technique** | 16 | needs something the animation library cannot do yet: travel across the floor (root motion), a gait cycle, inversion, or rolling and floor-to-standing transitions |
| **Partner** | 16 | needs a second figure (a grappling dummy or a training partner), which means a second rig |

Total 179. The app lists *Goblet Squat* twice (dumbbell and kettlebell), so it counts twice here and shares one classification.

## Suggested order

1. **Variants** (7): done.
2. **New, bodyweight** and **New, mace and kettlebell** (about 50): no new modelling. Many are floor poses (plank, bridge, dead bug) the Step 14 technique already covers.
3. **Dumbbell, then barbell** (35): one prop each with grips, then many exercises. These are the biggest payoff per prop.
4. **Fixtures** (13) and **small props** (10): a bench, box and wall unlock many at once.
5. **Technique** (16): each needs a new library feature first (root motion, a gait cycle, rolling). Do them last, one feature at a time.
6. **Machines** (17) and **Partner** (16): the most modelling for the least variety. Decide later whether the app needs them animated at all.
